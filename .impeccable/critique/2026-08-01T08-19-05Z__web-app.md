#### Overall Impression

The bones of an evidence folio are real: paper + brass, room heroes, walkthrough spine on the desk, Finish copy that wants to be a closing ritual. The product still behaves like **two review apps stapled to a pretty cover**, then asks a landlord to personally confirm **97** things. Against a £165 clerk, that is not a design polish problem — it is a promise problem. The single biggest opportunity: make the first five minutes answer “the whole property is here and the risky bits are covered,” then make human judgement rare, not the default chore.

#### What's Working

1. **Property-first room hero** on workspace overview — Hallway plate with Fraunces naming is the strongest brand moment.
2. **Evidence-desk claim stage** — “seen at” + Play this moment + walkthrough spine is Frame.io-grade signature craft.
3. **Finish ritual voice** — “Close the file calmly.” with a staged checklist is the right emotional end-state when properly gated.

#### Priority Issues

1. **[P0] Two products, one brand**
   - **What:** `/` field workspace and `/review` evidence desk use different shells, progress models (97 decisions vs 0/179 items), and Confirm UX; “Full evidence desk” is an escape hatch into a second mental map.
   - **Why it matters:** Completeness and closure cannot be felt across two apps; first-timers never learn the map; Morgan cannot trust a file they cannot hold in one head.
   - **Fix:** One folio shell. Modes: Orient → Decide → Power tools → Finish. Demote frame tools behind “Need frame tools,” never fork the product.
   - **Suggested command:** `$impeccable shape one-folio-shell`

2. **[P0] Judgment volume without triage**
   - **What:** First viewport repeats “97 decisions left”; overview still lists every hallway claim before the room atlas.
   - **Why it matters:** Completeness becomes a chore wall. Peace of mind loses to the clerk.
   - **Fix:** Default human queue to ~8–15 must-judge items (safety, meters, defects, low confidence, missing close-up). High-confidence routine auto-accepted with sample audit. Never put “97” as the emotional headline.
   - **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill review-queue`

3. **[P0] Evidence integrity failures on the decision card**
   - **What:** Decision surfaces can present a named claim beside context that does not depict it (agent observation: Ceiling paired with bathtub still); Confirm is still one thumb away.
   - **Why it matters:** Credibility is the product. One mismatch makes the schedule feel hallucinated.
   - **Fix:** Block confirm on weak/mismatched links; force re-link, Not visible, or scrub-to-moment; never present mismatched media as default proof.
   - **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden evidence-confirm`

4. **[P1] Orient phase still buried**
   - **What:** Overview shows one room hero + full evidence register; the 9-room index sits below 16 claim rows. docs/17’s room gallery is not the first viewport.
   - **Why it matters:** User cannot answer “did it get my whole property?” without scrolling past busywork.
   - **Fix:** Property atlas first — room plates with progress, defect heat, next action. Register becomes room-scoped after a plate is chosen.
   - **Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout overview-atlas`

5. **[P1] Closure without hard gates**
   - **What:** Finish allows Sign / tenant link while address empty and 97 open; report cover still “Address not yet specified.”
   - **Why it matters:** High-stakes end without guardrails is reassurance theatre, not calm closure.
   - **Fix:** Address required; explicit “Issue with N unresolved” acknowledgment; tenant link only after landlord sign criteria.
   - **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden finish-gates`
